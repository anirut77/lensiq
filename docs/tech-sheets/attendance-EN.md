## ใช้ทำอะไร (what it is for)

The two lines shown on the web page (decision 223-7):

- Staff clock in with a PIN or QR code on the shop's device, no camera needed. Face scan is an option only for staff who consent.
- With face scan, staff sometimes need to scan again or have a supervisor confirm, and it does not stop every fake image, so someone should watch the clock-in point.

## Known limits

- PIN/QR needs no camera; face scan only for staff who consent
- Face scan does not stop every fake image, so someone should watch the clock-in point
- Nothing has been measured at a real workplace yet; the figures above come from public face photos
