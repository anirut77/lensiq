## ใช้ทำอะไร (what it is for)

The two lines shown on the web page (decision 223-7):

- Makes profile and ID photos from your picture, changing the background and retouching on this device. (studio)
- Some images may show odd edges or seams; look at every image before use. Drawn outfits are still a draft and are off by default. (studio)
- An event photo booth with frames and stickers; print or take the file straight away. (photobooth)
- ID photos with a drawn outfit are still a draft and off by default; check each photo before printing. (photobooth)
- Converts PDF to Word on this device, with no upload. (converter)
- Documents with complex tables or images may not keep their layout. (converter)
