#### creative.html

~~~~text
[cr.s.acc] The attention map is a prediction from classic visual models, not real eye tracking. Measured on MIT1003 (703 images not used for tuning): AUC-Judd 0.83, NSS 1.59, CC 0.47 — better than guessing the image center (NSS 1.25), but below recent deep models
[cr.s.accl] Method and limitations
[cr.s.view.hint] Red = more predicted attention, blue = less; numbers 1–5 = predicted order the eye reaches (not measured eye tracking)
[cr.s.cmp.cav2] scores come from models and design rules; sales or clicks are not measured. Use it to shortlist, then run a real A/B test with viewers, for example by rotating on
[cr.s.m.sal.d] Combines two classic models that need no training data: Spectral Residual (Hou & Zhang 2007) and center-surround differences in luminance, color and edge orientation following Itti, Koch & Niebur (1998); detected faces and text are weighted up (people tend to look at both), and a bias toward the image center is added. Weights were tuned on 300 MIT1003 images and measured on a separate 703
[cr.s.m.cx.d] 70% from the share of edge pixels (Sobel), 30% from the number of colors used. This is a reference value, not calibrated against human ratings. JPEG file size per pixel is also shown, which Pieters, Wedel & Batra (2010) used to measure feature complexity
[cr.s.m.txt.d] Finds boxes with dense, line-like alternating light/dark edges (typical of text), splits the colors in each box into two groups (text/background) and computes contrast per WCAG 2.x. Lines taller than 4% of image height count as large text
[cr.s.m.face.d] Uses face-api TinyFaceDetector with 68 facial landmarks (same as other LENSIQ pages), estimates head turn from the nose tip relative to the midpoint between the eyes, then checks whether it points toward a marked area. Eye direction is not measured
[cr.s.m.cmp.d] Weighted: attention on key areas relative to their size 35%, key areas among the first 2 viewing points 20%, text contrast passing 20%, low clutter 15%, text amount 10%. A gap under 6 points counts as a tie
[cr.s.acc.h] Accuracy measurement (MIT1003)
[cr.s.acc.p] MIT1003 (Judd et al., 2009): 1,003 images, 15 viewers each, 3 seconds per image. Used only for measurement and not included in the system. Weights tuned on 300 images and measured on a separate 703, with the heatmap at 128 px on the long side
[cr.s.acc.note] AUC 0.5 = random, 1.0 = perfect; NSS 0 = random. Full and per-component results in ACCURACY.md §10. Ad and pack images differ from the everyday photos in MIT1003, so results on your creatives may vary
[cr.s.acc.m] Model
[cr.s.acc.r1] This page (all components)
[cr.s.acc.r2] Center guess only
[cr.s.acc.r3] Without center bias
[cr.s.acc.r4] Images with faces only (74)
[cr.s.acc.r5] Same images, no face weighting
~~~~

#### creative.html

~~~~text
[cr.s.acc] 注意力热图是基于经典视觉模型的预测值，并非真实眼动追踪。在 MIT1003 上测量（703 张未用于调参的图片）：AUC-Judd 0.83、NSS 1.59、CC 0.47 — 优于只猜画面中心（NSS 1.25），但低于新一代深度模型
[cr.s.accl] 测量方法与局限
[cr.s.view.hint] 红色 = 预计获得较多注意，蓝色 = 较少；数字 1–5 = 预计视线到达的顺序（并非真实眼动测量）
[cr.s.cmp.cav2] 分数来自模型和设计规则，并未测量销量或点击。请先用于筛选，再与真实观众做 A/B 测试，例如轮播展示于
[cr.s.m.sal.d] 结合两种无需训练数据的经典模型：Spectral Residual（Hou & Zhang 2007），以及依据 Itti、Koch & Niebur（1998）思路计算的亮度、颜色和边缘方向的中心-周边差异；对检测到的人脸和文字加权（人们常看这两类），并加入观看画面中心的倾向。权重用 MIT1003 的 300 张图调整，再在另外 703 张图上测量
[cr.s.m.cx.d] 70% 来自边缘像素比例（Sobel），30% 来自使用的颜色数量。这是参考值，未与真人评分校准。同时显示每像素 JPEG 文件大小，Pieters、Wedel & Batra（2010）用它衡量细节复杂度
[cr.s.m.txt.d] 寻找明暗边缘成行密集交替的框（文字特征），再把框内颜色分为两组（文字/背景），按 WCAG 2.x 计算对比度。行高超过图片高度 4% 时视为“大号文字”
[cr.s.m.face.d] 使用 face-api 的 TinyFaceDetector 和 68 个面部关键点（与 LENSIQ 其他页面相同），根据鼻尖相对双眼中点的位置估计头部转向，再判断是否朝向已标记区域。不测量眼球方向
[cr.s.m.cmp.d] 加权：重要区域获得的注意力相对其面积 35%，重要区域位于前 2 个观看点 20%，文字对比度达标 20%，不杂乱 15%，文字量 10%。相差不到 6 分视为不相上下
[cr.s.acc.h] 准确度测量（MIT1003）
[cr.s.acc.p] MIT1003（Judd 等，2009）：1,003 张图片，每张 15 名观看者，每张观看 3 秒。仅用于测量，未包含在系统中。用 300 张调整权重，在另外 703 张上测量，热图长边分辨率 128 像素
[cr.s.acc.note] AUC 0.5 = 随机，1.0 = 完美；NSS 0 = 随机。详细结果及各组件结果见 ACCURACY.md §10。广告和包装图片与 MIT1003 中的普通照片不同，用于您的素材时结果可能有差异
[cr.s.acc.m] 模型
[cr.s.acc.r1] 本页（全部组件）
[cr.s.acc.r2] 只猜画面中心
[cr.s.acc.r3] 不使用中心倾向
[cr.s.acc.r4] 仅含人脸的图片（74 张）
[cr.s.acc.r5] 同一批图片，不加人脸权重
~~~~
