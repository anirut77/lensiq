## ใช้ทำอะไร (what it is for)

The two lines shown on the web page (decision 223-7):

- Reads Thai licence plates on the device for car parks and villages. Staff confirm every car before the barrier opens or a fee is charged.
- On real camera images most cars still need staff to tap the plate or correct the number; tilted, dark, dirty or motorcycle plates may be misread.

## Known limits

- Staff confirm every car before the barrier opens or a fee is charged
- Tilted, dark, dirty or motorcycle plates may be misread
- Nothing has been measured at a real gate or car park yet; village auto-open needs a passed on-site test first

## Licences

- PP-OCRv5 Thai recogniser (Apache-2.0), LPD-YuNet plate finder (OpenCV Zoo); training-data rights in LICENSES/THIRD_PARTY.md
