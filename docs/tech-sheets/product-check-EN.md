## ใช้ทำอะไร (what it is for)

The two lines shown on the web page (decision 223-7):

- Reads medicine and product labels and barcodes and checks advert wording on this device. Photos never leave it.
- It may misread a medicine name or a number and may miss advert wording phrased differently. It is not a final check; always confirm with a pharmacist.
