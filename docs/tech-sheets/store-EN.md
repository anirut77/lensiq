## ใช้ทำอะไร (what it is for)

The two lines shown on the web page (decision 223-7):

- Counts people through the shop door, queues and an estimated conversion rate, identifying no one. (store)
- Groups or crowds at the door can be miscounted; compare with your own count before relying on it. (store)
- Brings the counts from the other tools into one place. (insights)
- Its numbers are only as good as the source tools. (insights)
