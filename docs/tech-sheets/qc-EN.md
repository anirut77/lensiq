The two lines shown on the web page (decision 223-7):

- Checks bottles and caps on the line with a fixed camera.

### Former page texts rendered from docs/accuracy-figures.json

- `qc.s5` (qc.html)
  - EN: Measured on simulated bottle images only, not yet on a real line: with a fixed camera and light it catches almost every simulated defect without rejecting good bottles; brightness changes of up to ±20% after calibration no longer reject good bottles (lighting is normalised every frame), and skewed labels are checked: 5° and 10° are rejected, 2° passes · with belt-stop jitter a 6 px cap lift is caught about 80% of the time. Scratches and dents are not inspected. Recalibrate when the light changes shape (a new shadow or reflection), and compare with manual inspection before enabling automatic rejection
  - 中文: 仅在模拟瓶子图像上测量，尚未在真实产线上测量：摄像头和光照固定时，几乎能检出所有模拟缺陷且不误判合格瓶；校准后亮度变化最多 ±20% 不再误判合格瓶（每帧做光照归一化），并会检查标签歪斜：歪 5° 和 10° 判为不合格，2° 通过 · 停位抖动时 6 px 的瓶盖抬高约 80% 能被检出。不检查划痕和凹痕。光照形态改变（新阴影、反光）时需重新校准，启用自动剔除前请与人工检验对比
- `cameras.accpilot` (cameras.html)
  - EN: The on-site accuracy check gives two levels: “pilot grade” uses relaxed targets with a 90% confidence interval and can be completed in one day (for example 50–100 line crossings, or 30 good + 25 bad bottles), to decide whether to continue the trial; “pass (confirmed)” uses the full targets with a 95% interval and needs far more data. A pilot-grade result is not an accuracy certification
  - 中文: 现场准确度检查给出两个等级：“试点级”采用放宽的目标和 90% 置信区间，一天内即可完成（例如越线 50–100 次，或合格瓶 30 个 + 不合格瓶 25 个），用于决定是否继续试用；“通过（已确认）”采用完整目标和 95% 置信区间，需要多得多的数据。试点级结果不是准确度认证
