## ใช้ทำอะไร (what it is for)

The two lines shown on the web page (decision 223-7):

- Tells invigilators which seat to check. The system only flags; a person decides every case. It reads no emotion and recognises no one. (exam)
- Phones close to the camera are detected well. A phone lying on the desk is usually missed, so invigilators should still walk the room. (exam)
- The system only assists the invigilator; it does not decide misconduct. (examlab)
- One camera watches several seats and flags empty seats, extra people and leaning so the invigilator can check. (examhall)
- Faces far from the camera are not found yet, and phones are seen only in the front rows near the camera. Invigilators should still walk the room. (examhall)

## Known limits

- The system only flags seats to check; it never decides misconduct, and invigilators still walk the room
- A phone lying on the desk is usually missed; faces far from the camera in the hall view are not found yet
- Nothing has been measured in a real exam room yet; the figures above come from public still images, not webcams or exam rooms
- Reads no emotion and recognises no one
