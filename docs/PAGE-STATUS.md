### Reviewer round (2026-11-09): checks added before the fixes

Written before `tools/cat-data.cjs` was changed for the independent review. The checks above stay as they are; these
are added:

- **Claims tied to their evidence** (`tests/unit/cat-pages.test.js`): every accuracy line with a number names either
  its row ids in the register of [`docs/ACCURACY-CLAIMS.md`](ACCURACY-CLAIMS.md) §4 (`acr`) or quoted fragments of
  its source document (`ev`). The rows / fragments must exist, and every percentage and every `n = …` in the English
  line must be one of the numbers in those rows or fragments (rounded to the precision shown). A changed number in
  the register or the source document therefore fails the test until the category pages follow.
- **n on every measured line:** every line labelled synthetic, public dataset or preliminary carries `n = …` in TH, EN
  and 中文 (S3 format), and a figure measured on a tuning set or on the model's own training data says "optimistic"
  in all three languages.
- **Support pages are not products:** directory, notice, information, legal and account pages get the status
  "support page" instead of a readiness status, and the totals count products and support pages separately.
- **Format:** the 7 generated pages have no added line over 200 characters, the CSP `<meta>` included: since commit
  7c47eba `tools/csp-meta.mjs` writes it one directive per line on every page (owner decision 2026-11-09, no exemption).
