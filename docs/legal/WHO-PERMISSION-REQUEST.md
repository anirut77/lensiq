**Subject:** Request for permission: commercial use of facts from WHO Medical Product Alerts (with links to WHO)

Dear WHO Permissions team,

I am the developer of LENSIQ (https://anirut77.github.io/lensiq/), a web app based in Thailand. We are building
"Product Check", a consumer-protection feature that helps people check medicines, supplements and cosmetics they hold.
The app will be offered commercially in future; it is not charging users yet.

We would like permission to show **short factual extracts** from WHO Medical Product Alerts when a product a user checks
matches an alert, for example:

- the alert number and date;
- the product name, batch / lot number and stated manufacturer, as given in the alert;
- a one-line statement of the issue (for example "falsified", "substandard"), in our own words;
- the countries where the product was reported;
- a **direct link to the WHO alert page**, so the user reads the full alert on who.int.

What we would and would not do:

- **Attribution:** every match shows "Source: World Health Organization, Medical Product Alert N°<n>, <date>" with the
  link.
- **No endorsement:** we would not use the WHO name or emblem in a way that suggests WHO endorses LENSIQ, and we would
  not use the emblem at all.
- **No copying of alert text or images:** we would store only the facts listed above in a small dated table, updated by
  hand from the alert pages; no alert text, PDFs or photographs.
- **No redistribution:** the table is not sold, offered for download or passed to third parties.
- **No medical advice:** the app never says a product is genuine or fake and always advises the user to ask a
  pharmacist or the national regulator.

Could you please tell us:

1. whether WHO grants permission for this use, and on what terms (licence, fee, attribution wording);
2. whether a machine-readable list of the alerts (for example a feed or a dataset) is available, and under what terms;
3. whether a different licence applies to the WHO INN lists, which we would like to use for ingredient names.

Thank you for your time. I am happy to send screenshots of the planned screen.
