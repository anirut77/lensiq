Calculation notes for the fortune pages

  • Taksa: The Thai day begins at dawn (06:00); Wednesday births after 18:00 count as Rahu; Maha Taksa periods: Sun 6, Moon 15, Mars 8, Mercury 17, Saturn 10, Jupiter 19, Rahu 12, Venus 21 years (108 years in total)
  • BaZi: The year changes at Li Chun, months change at the 12 "Jie" solar terms, days follow the 60-day cycle, and the Zi hour (23:00) counts as the next day; local time from the time-zone history of the birth date is used (you can choose a fixed offset or true solar time instead, and the result states the value used); solar-term times are accurate to within about 1 minute
  • Lek 7 Tua 9 Than: Lunar months are computed from new moons and the winter solstice (the month containing the solstice is month 1); in leap years month 8 is counted twice, Thai style; the official almanac may place leap months differently, so results are approximate; the zodiac year changes around Songkran
  • Life graph: "Self-care" is a prompt to look after body and mind, not a prediction of health or illness; curve levels are weights based on traditional texts, not real measurements
  • Birth chart: Planet positions are computed with standard astronomical formulas and checked against published tables; interpretations vary between schools
  • I Ching: Changing-line meanings are general interpretations by line position, not full line-by-line translations
  • Chinese zodiac: Chinese New Year and Lichun are computed with astronomical formulas (Beijing time)
  • Compatibility: Thai: birth-day pairs (friend, opposing, balancing, same element), year animals and birth-year elements of the Phrommachat text · BaZi: Day Master elements, the five stem combinations and day and year branch relations · Vedic: Ashtakoota from the Moon's nakshatra (Lahiri ayanamsa), out of 30 without Gana, because the sources disagree on its scores · Western: major aspects between the two charts.
  • Compatibility: Planet positions are computed on this device with astronomical formulas (Meeus and Don Cross's Astronomy Engine, MIT licence). Birth dates and names you enter are not stored or sent anywhere; they are gone when you close the page.
  • Feng shui: Content has been reorganized in a positive tone; interpretations vary between schools, especially for the Bagua map and remedies. Annual and monthly flying star results have been checked against published charts for 2024–2027

Planet positions use astronomical formulas (Jean Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, and Astronomy Engine). Our reading principles are on Our reading principles.

占卜页面的计算说明

  • 泰式大运: 泰历一天从早上六点开始；周三晚上六点后出生算作罗睺；大运年数：太阳六、月亮十五、火星八、水星十七、土星十、木星十九、罗睺十二、金星二十一（共一百零八年）
  • 八字: 年以立春为界，月以十二“节”为界，日按六十甲子循环，子时（23:00）算作次日；使用出生当日按时区历史的当地时间（也可选固定时差或真太阳时，结果会注明所用数值），节气时刻误差约一分钟以内
  • 七数九宫: 农历月份由朔日和冬至推算（含冬至的月份为一月），闰年按泰式把八月算两次；官方历书的闰月安排可能不同，因此是近似值；生肖年约在泼水节更换
  • 人生曲线: “自我照顾”只是提醒照顾身心，并不预测健康或疾病；曲线高低是按典籍给出的权重，不是实际测量
  • 生辰命盘: 行星位置采用标准天文公式计算，并对照已公布的表格校验；各派解释可能不同
  • 易经: 变爻含义为按爻位的通用解释，并非逐爻全文翻译
